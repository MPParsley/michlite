About MiCH lite

Innovation in equine dental illumination since 2012

Our Story

MiCH lite introduced the EDSL (Equine Dental Speculum Light) MiCHlite on the market in 2012 and has been continuously improving our products ever since.

Our main goal is to have satisfied customers, which we try to achieve by creating high-quality products that are safe, reliable, and very easy to use.

We build the world's smallest and most powerful illuminator for horse dental operations, helping veterinarians and equine dental practitioners deliver the best possible care.

The MiCH lite Team

Our Journey

2012

2014

Establishment of MiCH bvba as the official company, expanding production and distribution.

Continuous Improvement
