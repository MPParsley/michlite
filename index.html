Welcome to MiCH lite

Since 2012, MiCH lite has been the leading innovator in equine dental illumination technology. We introduced the EDSL (Equine Dental Speculum Light) MiCHlite on the market and have been continuously improving our product ever since.

Our main goal is to have satisfied customers, which we achieve by creating high-quality products that are safe, reliable, and very easy to use.

Why Choose MiCH lite?

Reliable

Tested on many horses across all breeds and disciplines, our products have proven their reliability and efficiency.

Safe

Designed with safety in mind for both the practitioner and the horse, ensuring worry-free operations.

Easy to Use

Simple and intuitive design that allows practitioners to focus on what matters most - the treatment.

High Quality

Premium materials and craftsmanship ensure long-lasting performance and durability.
